Design System Foundation

The foundation layer of the ANTZ design system: tokens, general components and guidance. The look comes from ANTZ Home Style 6: a soft green board, white cards, one strong green, Inter throughout.

Structure follows Shopify Polaris (foundations, tokens, components, patterns, content), GitHub Primer (functional colour tokens, light and dark from one set of names), Atlassian (surface and elevation tokens, guidance per component) and GOV.UK (plain words, "when to use / when not to use", accessible by default).

Start

Principles

Three rules taken from Style 6.

1White cards on a green board

Content sits on white. The board is soft mint. Depth comes from long soft shadows, not borders.

2Colour lives in the icon

Cards stay white; a tinted icon tile carries the colour. One strong colour per element.

3Quiet type, loud numbers

Inter 600 for labels, 300 to 400 for big numbers. Small sizes, tight tracking on headings.

Do
  • Keep content on white cards over the mint board.
  • Put colour in the icon tile, not the card.
  • Make the figure the loudest thing on a stat card.
Don't
  • Use more than one feature fill per screen.
  • Tint a whole card to show its category.

Primitives

Do
  • Use semantic tokens in components so dark mode works for free.
  • Keep cards white and put colour in the icon tile.
  • Use --color-accent-bright (lime) as a fill: bars, tags, highlights.
Don't
  • Put lime (#52F990) or coral (#FA6140) text on white. Both fail AA.
  • Use Style 6's grey #7A8684 for text. It is 3.8:1; use --color-text-muted.
  • Mix more than one strong colour in one card.

Contrast checks

Worked out live from the current theme. Text needs 4.5:1 (AA); large text (24px, or 19px bold) and UI parts such as borders, focus rings and icons need 3:1. Switch the theme to check dark.

Limits

  • One feature fill per screen: green, charcoal, blue, teal, mint, glow or aurora. Everything else is a white card.
  • Accent budget: strong colour (accent, solid status, feature fill) covers about a tenth of a screen at most. One primary button per view or card.
  • One strong colour per card. Tints and icon tiles carry the category; the card stays white.
  • Three status colours at most visible in one card, and one danger element unless the card lists errors.
  • Six hues at most in one chart or legend. Beyond that, group the rest as "Other" in neutral.

Dark mode

  • Swap by role, never by value: semantic tokens change between themes, primitives don't. Never hard-code a light hex.
  • Surfaces get lighter as they rise: board, canvas, card, raised. Shadows do less, so keep the step between surfaces.
  • The accent turns lime, and text on it turns dark green. Don't put white on the dark accent.
  • Text on tints uses the -ink tokens, which turn pastel in dark. Base hues for green, violet and navy lighten so icons and series keep 3:1.
  • Feature fills (deep green, charcoal, gradients) stay the same in both themes, and so does the white or lime text on them.
  • Check both columns of the table above before using a new pair.

Typography

Inter only, variable weight 300 to 700, loaded from Google Fonts. Body is 14px. Headings use 600 and slight negative tracking; large figures go light (300 to 400) and tight.

SampleTokenSize / weight / trackingUse
Do
  • Use font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums (.num) for counts and times.
  • Use sentence case everywhere.
Don't
  • Use uppercase except for the 11px overline.
  • Go below 11px, or use 300 weight below 28px.

Spacing

A 4px base with the half steps Style 6 actually uses (6, 10, 14, 18). Gaps inside a card are 8 to 14; card padding is 16 to 18; panel insets are 26.

Do
  • Use 8 to 14 for gaps inside a card and 16 to 18 for its padding.
  • Pick from --space-*, half steps included.
Don't
  • Use off-scale values like 15px or 22px.
  • Add space with empty elements; use gap.

Radii

Corners get rounder as things get bigger: 8 for chips, 10 for controls, 12 for rows and icon buttons, 16 for cards, 20 for dialogs, 22 for app panels. Pills and avatars are full.

Do
  • Round bigger things more: chips 8, controls 10, cards 16, sheets 20.
  • Keep --radius-full for pills, avatars and round buttons.
Don't
  • Mix two radii on one component.
  • Round a card less than the controls inside it.

Elevation

Style 6 shadows are long, soft and green-tinted, with a negative spread so they sit low under the card. Surfaces are flat colour, never borders plus shadow on the same card.

Do
  • Use --shadow-sm for cards and --shadow-lg for menus.
  • Let surface colour do the lifting in dark mode.
Don't
  • Give a card a border and a shadow.
  • Stack shadows on nested cards.

Motion

Fast and soft. Colour changes take 150ms; lifts and slides take 200 to 250ms on --ease-standard; things that open or slide a pill use --ease-spring at 350 to 500ms. Under prefers-reduced-motion every duration token drops to 0.

Card motion

Cards lift 3px with a shadow step on hover and press to 98%. They rise 14px and fade in as they scroll into view, 60ms apart. Figures count up the first time they show; period switches crossfade them. The status rail grows down from the top. Loading cards get a soft sweep. With reduced motion, all of it becomes an instant change.

Layout and breakpoints

Style 6 is an app frame: an 18px gutter, a white header bar, the main canvas and a 380px right panel, all at 22px corners. Three sizes: mobile, tablet and desktop. They replace Style 6's 640 / 760 / 1060 steps with 600 and 1024, which line up with phones, tablets held either way, and laptops. CSS variables can't go in media queries, so the --bp-* tokens document the numbers; use the numbers directly.

Voice and words

Plain, short and specific. The product talks like a capable colleague, not a brand.

How the product talks

  • Lead with the answer, then the detail: "Up 18% on last weekend. Tigers were busiest."
  • Say where facts came from: "From the footfall sensors · 2 enclosures".
  • Name people: "Sarah asked for this", never "a user".
  • Say what happens next and when: "Goes out today at 6pm".
  • Sentence case, no exclamation marks in UI text, no emoji in the interface.
  • Numbers as digits, times as 6pm or 18:00 (pick one per locale), currency with symbol: ₹48k.
