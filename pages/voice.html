Voice and words

How the product talks

  • Lead with the answer, then the detail: "Up 18% on last weekend. Tigers were busiest."
  • Say where facts came from: "From the footfall sensors · 2 enclosures".
  • Name people: "Sarah asked for this", never "a user".
  • Say what happens next and when: "Goes out today at 6pm".
  • Sentence case, no exclamation marks in UI text, no emoji in the interface.
  • Numbers as digits, times as 6pm or 18:00 (pick one per locale), currency with symbol: ₹48k.
