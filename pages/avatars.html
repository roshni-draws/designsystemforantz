Avatars

People are round: initials or a photo, with an optional presence dot. Tint them with the accent hues.

AS JR SK MP ASSKJR+4

Avatars

Do
  • Show initials when there is no photo.
  • Tint avatars with the accent hues so people stay apart in a list.
Don't
  • Use an avatar for a place or a thing; use an icon tile.
  • Show more than 4 avatars in a stack; end with "+N".
