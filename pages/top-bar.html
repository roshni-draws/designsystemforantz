Top bar and action bar

The phone header: back, a title and up to two actions, or back and a search. The action bar holds a form's actions at the bottom of the screen, with the main action taking the width.

Housing

Top bar and action bar

Do
  • Put back on the left and at most two actions on the right.
  • Pin the action bar when a form is longer than the screen.
Don't
  • Put a primary button in the top bar.
  • Put more than two buttons in the action bar.
