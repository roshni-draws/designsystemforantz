Cards

White is the default. Feature fills are for one hero card per screen, taken from Style 6's widgets.

  1. Header: icon tile, title, meta
  2. Trailing action: count, segmented or go-arrow
  3. Body: number, list or chart
  4. Footer link (optional)

Cards

Do
  • Use at most one feature fill per screen.
  • Make the whole card the link when it goes somewhere, and rotate the arrow on hover.
Don't
  • Put small white text on blue or teal.
  • Add a border and a shadow to the same card.
  • Run a stripe down a card's outer edge; put the rail beside the text.
Wrong
Outer edge stripeBends round the corner
