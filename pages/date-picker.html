Date picker

Weeks start on Monday.

Date picker

Weeks start on Monday. Today has a ring, the picked day is filled, days outside the month are dimmed and can't be picked. Arrow buttons move a month.

Date picker

Do
  • Start weeks on Monday and ring today.
  • Disable dates that can't be picked.
Don't
  • Let people pick days outside the month shown.
  • Mark the picked day with colour alone; set aria-pressed too.
