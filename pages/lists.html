Lists

A list is a header, rows and an optional footer.

Lists

A list is a header, rows and an optional footer. Rows are the list rows from the components above, with a trailing control: a chevron to open, a checkbox, radio or switch to choose, a plus to add or a cross to remove. Headers can collapse the list.

Lists

Do
  • Use one kind of trailing control per list.
  • Collapse long lists behind their header.
Don't
  • Put a chevron on a row that opens nothing.
  • Mix selection rows and navigation rows in one list.
