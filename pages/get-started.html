Getting started

Get started

Load two stylesheets, use the semantic tokens and the component classes, and the light and dark themes come for free.

The files

tokens.cssPrimitives, semantic tokens, light and dark themes.
components.cssEvery component on this page. Uses tokens only.
Docs siteindex.html, pages/ and assets/: this site.

Use it

<link href="https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Inter:wght@300..700&display=swap" rel="stylesheet">
<link rel="stylesheet" href="design-system-foundation/tokens.css">
<link rel="stylesheet" href="design-system-foundation/components.css">

<html data-theme="dark">   <!-- optional: force a theme; leave it off to follow the OS -->

Structure follows Shopify Polaris (foundations, tokens, components, patterns, content), GitHub Primer (functional colour tokens, light and dark from one set of names), Atlassian (surface and elevation tokens, guidance per component) and GOV.UK (plain words, "when to use / when not to use", accessible by default).

What these docs cover

  • Foundations. Values come from the Style 6 stylesheet. Anything not on Style 6 is marked derived.
  • Components. General building blocks. Every one shows its variants and its states.
  • Foundation components from the Figma file. What the Figma page "Foundational Components" adds to the components above. Most of its 2,000-odd variants are colour and state copies of a component that already exists here, so only the missing pieces are new.
  • Pattern components. Bigger pieces built from the components above, from the Figma page "Pattern components" and the headers of the screens: bars, the post card, attachments, priority, lists, the date picker, the animal card and progress.
  • Stat cards. Figures and the cards that hold them, from the Figma page "Stats cards" and the Housing screen. Numbers are semibold and tabular; the label sits under the number and says what was counted.
  • Screens. The Figma "screens" page, rebuilt from the components on this page, each with a mobile, tablet and desktop layout. Switch the preview size: frames are 390, 820 and 1280px wide, scaled to fit, and their content scrolls inside the frame.
  • Content. Plain, short and specific. The product talks like a capable colleague, not a brand.

Get started

Do
  • Load tokens.css before components.css.
  • Use semantic tokens and component classes in your own CSS.
Don't
  • Copy values out of the CSS as raw hex.
  • Change a primitive to restyle one screen.
