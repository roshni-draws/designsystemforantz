Two side-nav treatments: light (Style 6) and deep green. Use one per product.

Bottom navigation

Five destinations at most. On a phone it is a bottom bar; inside a .shell on a tablet or desktop it becomes a side rail (see Responsive). The current one gets a soft pill behind its icon and a semibold label. Labels stay visible.

App bar, side nav and dock

Do
  • Mark the current page with aria-current.
  • Keep the side nav to the product's main areas.
Don't
  • Use both side nav treatments in one product.
  • Put more than one dock on a screen.

Bottom navigation

Do
  • Keep 3 to 5 destinations, each with a label.
  • Show the current one with the soft pill and aria-current.
Don't
  • Hide labels to fit more items.
  • Put actions in the bar; use a FAB or the action bar.
