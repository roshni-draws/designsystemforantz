Selection controls

Do
  • Use a switch only when the change applies at once.
  • Use a choice card when each option needs a line of explanation.
Don't
  • Use checkboxes for a pick-one question.
  • Disable an option without saying why.

Option groups

Do
  • Put the error under the whole group, not under one option.
  • Mark a required group in its legend.
Don't
  • Make a group of one; use a single checkbox.
  • Mix radios and checkboxes in one group.
