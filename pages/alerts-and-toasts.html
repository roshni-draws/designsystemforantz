Alerts and toasts

An alert stays on the page until the problem is gone. A toast confirms something just happened and leaves after about 4 seconds; it never holds the only copy of important information.

Quiet hours are onNotifications are paused until 07:00.
Instagram connectedScheduled posts will wait for your review.
Climate sensor offlineReptile house data is 2 hours old.
Couldn't send the reportEmail bounced. Check Sarah's address and try again.
Post approved. Goes out at 6pm. Couldn't reach Slack.

Alerts and toasts

Do
  • Keep an alert until the problem is fixed.
  • Offer Undo in a toast when the action can be reversed.
Don't
  • Put the only copy of an error in a toast.
  • Show more than one toast at a time.
