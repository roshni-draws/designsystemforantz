Text fields

Label above, hint below, error replaces the hint. Fields are white with a 3:1 border (Style 6 uses a fainter fill; the border was darkened to meet AA for controls).

  1. Label, 13px semibold
  2. Field, 44px min, 10px corners
  3. Hint or error, 12px
  4. Optional or required marker

Field additions

From the Figma field set: a leading icon, a trailing icon button, a filled style, a large size, success and warning tones, a label with a help button, and the floating label that Figma calls "filled-out".

Scan the ear tag or type the name.
Filled style, for use on white cards.
Large: 52px, for the main field on a screen.
Within the normal range.
This enclosure is planned for 8.

Figma's eight other field colours (teal, sky, navy and so on) are left out on purpose: a field's colour should say its state, not its category.

Search bar

The Figma "styled field": a pill with a menu button, the query and trailing search and scan buttons. Use it once, at the top of a home screen. The search row pairs the plain search with a filter button.

Text fields

Do
  • Say what's wrong and how to fix it: "Enter an email like name@antz.ai".
  • Link the error with aria-describedby and set aria-invalid.
Don't
  • Use the placeholder as the label.
  • Show errors before the person has left the field.

Field additions

Do
  • Use the floating label where space is tight, such as a date in a card.
  • Pair a success or warning border with a hint that says why.
Don't
  • Colour a field by category, such as teal or navy.
  • Use the filled style on the mint board; it needs a white card.

Search bar

Do
  • Use the pill search once, at the top of a home screen.
  • Keep its trailing actions to two: search and scan.
Don't
  • Use the pill as a form field.
  • Show a valid or invalid state without saying what was found.
