Site card

One site per card: name and four figures on a feature fill, the site lead and quick contact below. A warning icon next to a label flags figures that need a look. The Figma file uses photos as covers; here the cover is the green or charcoal fill, and a photo can sit behind it later.

Site card

Do
  • Flag a figure with the warning icon and a screen-reader note.
  • Name the person in the call and message buttons.
Don't
  • Put more than four figures on a cover.
  • Use the blue or teal fill as a cover; small white text fails there.
