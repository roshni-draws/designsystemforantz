Chips and tabs

Chips filter or toggle and can be many at once.

Chips and segmented controls

Chips filter or toggle and can be many at once. A segmented control picks one view; it doubles as tabs (use role="tablist" and aria-selected).

Pills and chips

Pills are round chips for filters and picked values. A picked value can carry an avatar and a remove button; a person or team can carry a second line. Selected is always the accent: Figma's ten chip colours are not kept.

Tabs and figure tabs

Line tabs switch the period on a card. Figure tabs put a count over each label (Figma "Number w/ label"), so the tab doubles as a summary.

Chips and segmented controls

Do
  • Use chips to filter a list in place.
  • Use a segmented control for 2 to 4 views of the same thing.
Don't
  • Use a chip as the main action.
  • Put more than 4 options in a segmented control.

Pills and chips

Do
  • Name the item in every remove button's aria-label.
  • Use a person chip when a team needs its site under the name.
Don't
  • Colour pills by category; selected is always the accent.
  • Wrap a chip label onto two lines.

Tabs and figure tabs

Do
  • Use line tabs inside a card to switch the period.
  • Use figure tabs when the count is the reason to switch.
Don't
  • Use figure tabs for more than 4 options.
  • Put line tabs straight on the mint board.
