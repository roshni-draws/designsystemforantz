Post card

The Figma "Placeholder Card": an announcement or an important notice in the team feed. Important posts get a rail beside the text. Header says who and when; body has the title, up to three lines of text and an optional photo; footer holds reactions and who has seen it.

  1. Header: icon tile or avatar, title, meta, status, more
  2. Meta line: author, where it applies
  3. Title and text, clamped to 3 lines
  4. Media (optional)
  5. Footer: reactions, seen by or "Responded"

Post card

Do
  • Mark important posts with a status chip and the rail beside the text.
  • Clamp text to three lines; open the full post on tap.
Don't
  • Run a stripe down the card's outer edge.
  • Use the important style for routine posts.
Wrong
Outer edge stripeBends round the corner
