Motion

Fast and soft.

Motion

Fast and soft. Colour changes take 150ms; lifts and slides take 200 to 250ms on --ease-standard; things that open or slide a pill use --ease-spring at 350 to 500ms. Under prefers-reduced-motion every duration token drops to 0.

Card motion

Cards lift 3px with a shadow step on hover and press to 98%. They rise 14px and fade in as they scroll into view, 60ms apart. Figures count up the first time they show; period switches crossfade them. The status rail grows down from the top. Loading cards get a soft sweep. With reduced motion, all of it becomes an instant change.

Motion

Do
  • Use the duration and distance tokens so reduced motion turns them off.
  • Use spring easing only for things that open or slide.
  • Keep card motion to a lift, a press and one entrance.
Don't
  • Animate layout on every hover.
  • Run interface motion longer than 500ms.
  • Count a figure up again on every tab change; crossfade it instead.

Motion

Use the motion tokens so reduced motion turns movement off.

.thing { transition: transform var(--duration-base) var(--ease-standard); }
.thing:hover { transform: translateY(var(--motion-lift)); }
.thing:active { transform: scale(var(--motion-press)); }

/* Entering: --duration-enter, --ease-out, --stagger, --motion-rise */
